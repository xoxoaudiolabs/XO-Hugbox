Hugbox guide

Knobs are numbered 1-4 from the left. Pads are named by their keyboard key: the top row is 1234, then Q-R, A-F, Z-V. Shift is the orange key (or your Shift key).

How do I…?

Turn on chord mode

  1. Shift + S (SYN) picks the synth track and its PLAY screen.
  2. Hold Shift and turn knob 3 (CHRD): 5TH, TRIAD, 7TH, SUS2 or SUS4.
  3. Play pads: each one now plays a chord built from the current scale. Sequencer steps play chords too.
  4. Inversions: Shift + 3 (SND) until the title reads CHORD/ARP, then knob 2 (INV).

Turn on the arpeggiator

  1. On the synth PLAY screen, hold Shift and turn knob 4 (ARP): UP, DOWN, UP/DN, RAND or ORDER.
  2. Hold one or more pads; the arp plays their notes (with chord mode on, it plays the chord's notes).
  3. Speed, octaves, gate and latch are on SOUND page CHORD/ARP: Shift + 3 until you see it. RATE 1/4…1/32 incl. triplets, OCTS 1-3, AGAT note length, LTCH ON keeps it running after you let go.

Make a drum beat

  1. Shift + A (DRUM), then Shift + 2 (SEQ).
  2. Knob 1 (PAD) chooses which drum you're writing for: KCK, SNR, CHH…
  3. Tap pads to switch steps on/off (top-left = step 1, reading like text).
  4. Shift + D (RUN) starts and stops it. Pick another drum with knob 1 and add its steps.

Record by playing

  1. Shift + F (REC) arms recording (red dot on the top line), Shift + D (RUN) starts.
  2. Go to PLAY (Shift + 1) and play pads: each hit is written to the nearest step.

Tweak a single step

  1. In SEQ, hold a step pad and turn a knob: 1 VEL, 2 NOTE, 3 GATE (length), 4 PROB (chance it plays).

Change tempo, swing or pattern length

  • In SEQ: knob 2 BPM, knob 3 SWNG, knob 4 LEN (16, 32, 48 or 64 steps). Pages 1-4: Shift + Q W E R.

Change scale, key or octave

  • Synth PLAY screen, hold Shift: knob 1 OCT, knob 2 SCAL (CHROM, MAJOR, MINOR, PENTA, DORIAN). Root note: CHORD/ARP page, Shift + knob 4 ROOT.

Shape the synth sound

  • Shift + 3 (SND) cycles OSC (waves, detune, mix, FM, drift, glide) → FILTER (cutoff, resonance, envelope, volume, ADSR) → CHORD/ARP.

Change a drum sound

  • On the drum track, tap the pad, then Shift + 3 (SND): PTCH DCY CUT LVL, with Shift SMPL PAN CHOK KIT.

Add delay, drive or lo-fi

  • Shift + 4 (FX): knob 3 DLY (amount), 1 TIME (1/32…1/2, synced), 2 FDBK, 4 DRV; with Shift CRSH (bits), LOFI, LCUT (low cut), MVOL.

Record a sound onto a pad

  1. Click Use mic (Mic · samples, below). On the Hugbox, the mic is the small hole marked MIC.
  2. Shift + hold F (REC) for a moment: the SAMPLE view opens with an input meter. (A quick tap is still sequencer record.)
  3. Hold a pad, make the sound, let go. Up to 20 s. The take is trimmed to the sound and plays back once; the pad now shows U01…U16 on every screen.
  4. Too quiet or clipping? Shift + knob 1 GAIN (the meter shows while you turn it). To start only when the sound does: SETTINGS, Shift + knob 2 THRS.
  5. Leave the view (Shift + any view) and the samples are saved.

Edit a sample

  • In SAMPLE, tap a recorded pad: its waveform fills the screen (the same editor as XOXO Studio's). Knobs: 1 STRT, 2 END (the two markers), 3 FDIN, 4 FDOU (fades); with Shift GAIN, NORM (to -6 dB), REV (reverse) and SRC: turn it to KIT to put the built-in sound back (back to USER undoes it until you leave SAMPLE). Pitch, decay and filter are the pad's usual SOUND knobs.

Save or load a project

  1. Shift + V (SET). The top two pad rows are slots S1-S8 (filled = has a project).
  2. Hold a slot pad until the bar fills to save into it; tap a slot to load it.
  3. You never lose work by switching off: everything autosaves a few seconds after each change.

Copy a page, clear, mute, tap tempo

  • On the SETTINGS pads: CPY copies the current sequencer page of the current track, PST pastes it onto the page you're on (pick pages with Shift + Q-R), CLR clears the page, CLT (hold) clears the whole track.
  • MDR / MSY mute the drum / synth sequence (live pads still play).
  • TAP 3-4 times in time sets the tempo. PNC stops every stuck note (MIDI panic).

Play other gear over MIDI

  1. Shift + C (LAYERED) plays the Hugbox and sends MIDI; Shift + X (CONTROLLER) sends MIDI only.
  2. Synth notes go out on MCH, drums on DCH (10): SETTINGS knobs 1-2. The sequencer, arp and clock go out too.
  3. In Controller mode the knobs send CC 70-73 (Shift: 74-77) instead of changing sounds.
  4. Over Bluetooth: BLE ON (SETTINGS knob 4), then pair "XO-Hugbox" in your app.

Sync to a DAW or another box

  • SETTINGS knob 3 SYNC: INT = Hugbox is the clock; USB / BLE = follow incoming clock and start/stop (tempo shows on the screen; RUN is then controlled from outside).

Get back to where I was

  • Double-tap Shift to see every shortcut on the screen, then tap the pad you want.

  • Hold Shift: the knobs switch to their second four (the bottom line of the screen turns inverted). The screen stays put.
  • Double-tap Shift: this map appears on the OLED; tap any pad to run its shortcut. Shift, a knob or 5 s closes it.
  • SOUND pressed again cycles the synth pages. Choosing DRUM/SYNTH from FX or SETTINGS jumps to that track's PLAY screen.
Reading the screen
  • Top line: mode box (STD/CTL/LAY) · track · tempo · sequencer page shown (P1) · ▶ running / ■ stopped · ● record armed (blinks while recording) · BT Bluetooth on · battery.
  • Bottom line: what knobs 1-4 do; inverted while Shift is held. The small bar under each shows its value.
  • Turning a knob pops up its full name and value. < TURN / TURN > means the knob hasn't reached the stored value yet: turn it that way until it catches (so values never jump).
  • Pad grid: PLAY shows drum names or note names (root notes underlined, last-hit drum underlined). SEQ shows steps: filled = on, small block = playhead, dot = beyond the pattern length.
The views
  • PLAY: drums: 16 sounds, bottom-left is the kick. Synth: notes of the scale, bottom-left is the root, rising left→right, bottom→top.
  • SEQ: 16 steps per page, 4 pages. Drum steps belong to the drum chosen with knob 1; synth steps play scale notes.
  • SOUND: drum: the selected pad's sound. Synth: OSC, FILTER and CHORD/ARP pages.
  • FX: master delay, drive, bitcrush, lo-fi, low cut and volume.
  • SETTINGS: pads = projects (S1-S8), page copy/paste/clear, track mutes, tap tempo, panic. Knobs: synth MIDI channel, drum MIDI channel, clock source, Bluetooth; Shift: screen brightness, mic start threshold (OFF = start on the press), mic monitor.
  • SAMPLE (Shift + hold REC): record the mic onto pads and edit them. Filled pads hold a recording.

Mic · samples

The board's INMP441 mic listens through the small hole marked MIC between the keys and the knobs. Here your computer's mic stands in for it (turned down 20 dB, about as sensitive as the INMP441). Press Shift, hold F (REC), let go of Shift and keep holding: after 2 s it beeps, then records onto the selected pad until you let go of F. In SAMPLE, holding a pad does the same onto that pad. Use headphones if you turn the monitor on.
